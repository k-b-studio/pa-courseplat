Rules for every page from now on: (1) two-column layouts use `minmax(0,1fr)` for the main column and a fixed, capped sidebar track (editor: `minmax(0,1fr) 22rem`); (2) a grid that holds text declares its columns with `minmax(0,1fr)` tracks (`grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]` for one column), never an implicit `auto` track; (3) grid and flex children that hold text get `min-w-0`, and fixed pieces beside them (chips, icons, buttons) get `shrink-0`; (4) user, AI and caution text, labels and URLs wrap with `wrap-anywhere` (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), never `break-all` (it cuts Thai mid-syllable) and never `truncate` where the text sizes a column, so use `line-clamp-N` to shorten; (5) tab bars use equal `minmax(0,1fr)` columns with short labels (the full name goes in `aria-label` and `title`); (6) wide data (the course matrix, the header menu) may scroll inside its own box (`overflow-x-auto`), and the page itself never scrolls sideways (no `overflow: hidden` on html or body to hide it). `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` checks this at 390, 1024, 1140, 1280 and 1440 px in WebKit and Chromium.
